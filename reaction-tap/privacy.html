Privacy Policy — Reaction Tap

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Reaction Tap ("the game") is made by Briene Games (Cas Briene, the Netherlands). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and what you can do about it.

Contact: groundworkcode@outlook.com

1. What data we use

DataWhyWhere it is stored
Your highscores and settings (best score per mode and difficulty, your privacy choices) So you keep your highscores and unlocked modes Only on your own device. We never receive them.
Gameplay statistics (only if you tick "Share anonymous play statistics"): a random ID created by the game, when and how long you play, which mode and difficulty you choose, the level and score you reach, whether you use the ad retry, basic device information (model, operating system, language, country from your IP address) To understand how the game is played, so we can improve difficulty and fix problems. We only see totals and averages. Unity Analytics (Unity Gaming Services)
Advertising data (only when you choose to watch a video ad): your device's advertising ID, basic device information (model, operating system, language, approximate location from your IP address) and how you interact with the ad To show a video ad when you ask for one (to get one more try after a mistake), to count views and to prevent fraud. Ads are only based on your interests if you say yes when the game asks; otherwise you see general ads. Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with (such as Unity Ads)

The game has no account, no login and no online save. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location or photos. Video ads are always optional: you only see one when you tap the button to watch it.

2. Who processes the data

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Analytics) processes the gameplay statistics on our behalf, only with your consent. See unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies / ironSource) and the ad networks connected to it show the optional video ads. See unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Google Play distributes the game. See policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else.

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Highscores and settings: stored only on your device, needed for the game to work.
  • Gameplay statistics: only with your consent. The box is off unless you tick it. You can withdraw your consent at any time with Privacy settings on the start screen; the game then stops sending statistics.
  • Personalized ads: only with your consent, asked the first time you open the game. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings on the start screen. Without consent you get non-personalized ads, still only when you choose to watch one.

4. How long we keep it

Your highscores and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Gameplay statistics are kept by Unity Analytics for at most 24 months and are not linked to your name. For data processed by the ad networks, see their privacy policies above.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to see or delete your gameplay statistics, or ask us questions about your data, by emailing groundworkcode@outlook.com, or contact the ad partners through their privacy policies. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads). You can also file a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

6. Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, please contact us.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
