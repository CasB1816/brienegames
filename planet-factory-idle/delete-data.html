Delete your data — Planet Factory Idle

Briene Games · Last updated: 26 October 2026

You can ask us to delete your Planet Factory Idle account and all data linked to it at any time.

In the game

Open Settings and tap Delete my data, then confirm. This deletes your progress, your player name and your alliance membership, on your device and online. It can't be undone.

By email

If you no longer have the game installed, send an email to groundworkcode@outlook.com with the subject "Delete my data – Planet Factory Idle" and your player name or player ID (shown in the game's settings). We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous player ID and sign-in data
  • Your game progress and the online backup
  • Your player name
  • Your alliance membership, role and the link between you and what you contributed

What is kept

  • Alliance totals you contributed to (for example the level of the alliance headquarters) stay with the alliance, but are no longer linked to you.
  • Purchase records kept by Google Play (see Google's privacy policy). We only keep what tax and accounting law requires.

See also the privacy policy.
