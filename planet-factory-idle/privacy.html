Privacy Policy — Planet Factory Idle

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Planet Factory Idle ("the game") is made by Briene Games (Cas Briene, the Netherlands). This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it simple: we only use what the game needs to work.

Contact: groundworkcode@outlook.com

1. What data we use

DataWhyWhere it is stored
An anonymous player ID (created automatically when you first open the game) To save your progress online and to let you take part in alliances Unity Gaming Services

Ads are only based on your interests if you say yes when the game asks; otherwise you see general ads. Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with (such as Unity Ads)

We do not collect your real name, address, phone number, contacts, precise location or photos. Video ads are always optional: you only see one when you tap a button to watch it.

2. Who processes the data

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: sign-in, cloud save and server code) processes data on our behalf. See unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Google distributes the game and handles purchases. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies / ironSource) and the ad networks connected to it show the optional video ads. See unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

We do not sell your data. Apart from the ad partners above (only when you watch an ad), we do not share it with anyone else.

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Saving your progress, player name and alliance data: needed to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract).
  • Personalized ads: only with your consent (asked before your first video ad), which you can change at any time. Without consent you get non-personalized ads, still only when you choose to watch one.
  • Purchases: needed to deliver what you bought, and to meet legal obligations (for example accounting).

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data as long as you play. If you ask us to delete your data, we delete your player ID, progress, player name and your membership of an alliance within 30 days. Alliance totals that you contributed to (for example the level of the alliance headquarters) stay with the alliance, but are no longer linked to you. For data processed by the ad networks, see their privacy policies above.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop using it, by emailing groundworkcode@outlook.com (mention your player name or player ID). See how to delete your data. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads). You can also file a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

6. Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and you want their data removed, please contact us.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored by Unity. Only the game's server code can change alliance data.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
